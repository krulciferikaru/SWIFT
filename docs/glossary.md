# Glossary

The same word always means the same thing on every screen. Where two words could be confused, this page says which is which.

| Word on the screens | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Mobile number** | The subscriber's mobile phone number. It is what they sign in with and where texts (reminders, verification codes) are sent. Staff accounts sign in with an email instead. |
| **Service status** | A subscriber's billing state: **Active** (paid up), **Unpaid** (behind on payments) or **Disconnected** (three or more months behind). It follows payments automatically; staff can also set it by hand. Shown on Subscribers and the subscriber's own page. |
| **Account status** | Whether a *login* is allowed: **Pending** (waiting for approval), **Active**, **Inactive** (switched off) or **Rejected**. Shown on Manage Roles and the Approvals page. A subscriber can have an Active account and an Unpaid service. |
| **Availability** (of a plan) | **Offered**: the plan can be given to subscribers. **Not offered**: it is still billed for subscribers already on it, but cannot be given to new ones. This is different from archiving a plan. |
| **Service history** | The list of every time a subscriber's service status changed, with the date and whether staff or the system made the change. Subscribers can see their own. |
| **Installation date** | The day service was installed. The first bill is for the month *after* it. |
| **Balance** | What the subscriber owes now, in pesos. |
| **Months behind** | How many months are not fully paid. |
| **Advance credit** | Money paid beyond what is owed so far; it covers future months. |
| **OR number** | The number on the paper official receipt, recorded with each payment. |
| **Cancelled payment** | A payment that was entered wrongly and then cancelled by an administrator. It stays in the history (struck through, with the reason) but does not count. It is never deleted or edited. |
| **Payment receipt** | A printable summary made by the system for a recorded payment. It is not a BIR-registered official receipt. |
| **Archive** | Hides a subscriber or plan without deleting anything. It can be undone straight after, or restored later from the Archive page. |
| **Delete my account** | What a subscriber does to delete their SWIFT login. Only the login goes: the company keeps its record of them (marked *Deleted their account*), and sends no more texts. Different from *Delete permanently*, which staff do to an archived record. |
| **Delete permanently** | Removes an archived subscriber and their payments for good. It asks for the person's password and cannot be undone. |
| **Verified** | The mobile number was confirmed by a texted code. |
